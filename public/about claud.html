About StrangerChat

StrangerChat is a small, independent site that lets adults chat with random people by text or video. It is free, works in the browser, and does not ask for an account.

We care about keeping it respectful. That is why we have an 18+ gate, a Report button, automatic filters and rate limits. Questions or problems? Use the Contact link on the home page.
