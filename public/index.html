Staying safe

  • StrangerChat is for adults, 18 and older only.
  • Never share your full name, address, phone number or passwords.
  • Press Report if someone breaks the rules, then press New.
  • You do not need an account (Google sign-in is optional). Chat messages are not saved on our server.
  • An automatic filter blocks links and sexual or spam messages, and people reported by several users are blocked.
  • Video goes directly between browsers and is encrypted in transit. You can end any chat instantly.

Questions

Is StrangerChat free?

Yes. Text chat and video chat are free.

Can children use it?

No. It is only for people aged 18 and older.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Open the website in Chrome or Safari and start chatting.

How do interests work?

If someone with a matching interest is waiting, you are paired with them first. If nobody matches, you are paired with the next person.

Why is the other person's video blank?

Allow camera and microphone when your browser asks, close other apps using the camera, then press New. On some phones you may need to tap the video to start it.

How do I report someone?

Press Report in the chat, then press New to leave.
