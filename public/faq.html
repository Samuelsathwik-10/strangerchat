Frequently asked questions

Is StrangerChat free?

Yes. Text and video chat are free.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Google is optional.

Who can use it?

Only people aged 18 and older.

How do I report someone?

Press Report in the chat, choose a reason, and you are matched with someone new. People reported by several users are blocked.

Why is the video blank?

Allow camera and microphone in your browser, close other apps that use the camera, then press New. On some phones you need to tap the video once to start it.

Are chats saved?

Messages are not saved on our server.

Can I install it as an app?

Yes. Tap Install app on the home page, or use Add to Home Screen in your browser menu.
