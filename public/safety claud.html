Safety tips for chatting with strangers

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Never share your full name, address, school, workplace, phone number or passwords.
  • Do not click links or move to another app if a stranger asks you to.
  • Do not send photos or money.
  • Cover or move your camera if you are not comfortable.
  • Press Report for nudity, harassment, spam or someone who seems under 18, then press New.
  • If you ever feel unsafe, close the page. You can always leave.

If you are worried about a child, contact your local police or child protection service.
